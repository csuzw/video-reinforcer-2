Fundamental plan is to use a raspberry pi.  It will have 2 monitors with speakers connected via hdmi.  It will be controlled by an Elgato Stream Deck.  Buttons will be available on the Stream Deck that when pressed will launch full screen video with sound on a specific monitor (sound will also come from that monitor only).  When not displaying video, it should be black screen and silent.  Videos need to launch instantaneoudsly.  Can have arbitrary number of buttons for a screen with different associated videos.  Images for buttons.

Nice to have: support arbitrary number of monitors based on number of hdmi slots.  Would be cool if could be effectively an OS with videos/button config on SD card and easily editable if plugged in to another computer - or dual boot - so can edit with Raspberry PI normal OS but for actual use boots to this app OS.

I'd say the app is pretty fine as is - it's designed as a reinforcer during video reinforcement audiometry  (basic idea here; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FP3nzh65WhE)

- Allows users to add buttons, that launch videos on 1 or 2 screens, full screen with sound
- You can label the buttons and assigning them a hot key to launch the video
- You can choose whether the video launches on screen 1 or 2
-is basically an instantaneous video launch, no delays etc to ensure child properly rewarded

In the current version, you can also select L/R sound channel (important as reinforcing for a child turn.)

The only thing I'd really wonder that could be different, would be if different HDMI outputs could be used for the video sound instead of the left/right channel and the user could select them (i.e. left or right monitor with speaker build in via HDMI) - at the moment it's a less elegant solution of splitting to phono then running into a monitor line in - but that means the monitor also needs to be fed by VGA to look for the line in port and those kinds of monitors are getting a bit thin on the ground that also have speakers. In a new version, you'd just have two monitors with build in speakers connected with HDMI and then select the output for each side as you needed it.
